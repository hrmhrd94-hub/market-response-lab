Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

Market Response Lab is an independent research initiative focused on market-response experiments, content testing, and commerce decision systems. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled through this public website and through future authorized third-party integrations operated by the account owner.

2. Information we may process

This public website is primarily informational. Market Response Lab does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through this website. Hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request timestamps, and basic usage or security logs as part of operating the service.

3. Authorized third-party integrations

Where Market Response Lab connects to a third-party platform through an official API or authorization flow, the integration will access only the data and permissions explicitly authorized by the account owner and required for the relevant function. Examples may include publishing the account owner's own content, reading the account owner's boards or posts, and retrieving analytics for the account owner's content.

We do not claim access to other users' accounts unless such access is separately and explicitly authorized in the future. Third-party platforms remain governed by their own privacy policies, terms, and security practices.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to operate authorized publishing workflows, retrieve permitted analytics, evaluate market-response experiments, improve future content or product hypotheses, maintain service reliability, and meet security or legal obligations.

5. Sale and sharing of personal information

Market Response Lab does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for unrelated advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to host, secure, or operate the relevant service or authorized integration.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including research continuity, operational integrity, security, and applicable legal requirements. Data obtained through a third-party API may also be subject to that platform's retention and deletion requirements.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information under our control. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices

Where an integration relies on account authorization, the account owner may revoke access using the applicable third-party platform's account or authorization settings. Requests concerning information under Market Response Lab's direct control can be submitted through the official service interface until a dedicated public contact address is published.

9. Children's privacy

Market Response Lab is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

Market Response Lab. Contact information will be provided through the official service interface until a dedicated public contact address is published.
